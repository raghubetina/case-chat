# Takeout platform terms

- Takeout would be offered from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm on Fridays.
- The platform forecasts about 40 orders over the evening, with a mean order
  value of $43.
- The platform expects to bring 75% of the orders and charges a 28% commission
  on those orders.
- Packaging costs Vesta $1.55 per order.
- Guests receive a quoted ready time of 25 minutes.
- Takeout tips average about 8%.
- The platform demotes restaurants whose orders frequently miss the quoted
  ready time. Orders that run far past the quote are refunded and the food is
  discarded.
- The merchant dashboard has a control that pauses new orders.
